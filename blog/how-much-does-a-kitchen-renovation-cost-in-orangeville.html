How Much Does a Kitchen Renovation Cost in Orangeville? (2026 Guide)

The kitchen is almost always the room homeowners want updated first, and it's also the one with the widest cost range. Here's how kitchen renovation budgets typically break down for Orangeville homes in 2026, so you can figure out roughly where your project lands before requesting a quote.

Budget Refresh: $15,000–$25,000

This tier covers refacing or painting existing cabinets, new countertops, a new backsplash, updated hardware and lighting, and possibly a new sink and faucet — without moving plumbing or electrical. It's the fastest way to modernize a kitchen that has a good layout but dated finishes.

Mid-Range Renovation: $30,000–$60,000

This is where most full kitchen renovations in Orangeville land. It typically includes new cabinetry (not just refacing), stone or quartz countertops, new flooring, updated electrical for under-cabinet lighting or additional outlets, and possibly minor layout changes like relocating an island or opening a pass-through. Appliance upgrades are often bundled in at this level too.

High-End / Full Gut Renovation: $65,000+

A full gut renovation means removing the kitchen to the studs — moving plumbing and gas lines, reconfiguring the layout entirely, custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, and premium finishes throughout. This tier is common when a kitchen's layout genuinely doesn't work for how a family lives, or as part of a larger whole-home renovation.

What Drives Cost Up

Moving plumbing or gas lines (for an island sink or gas range relocation), structural changes like removing a wall between kitchen and dining room, custom cabinetry versus semi-custom or stock, and high-end appliance packages are the biggest cost drivers. Older Orangeville homes sometimes also need electrical panel upgrades to support a modern kitchen's power demands, which adds to the budget but is often worth addressing while walls are already open.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Orangeville?

Most kitchen renovations in Orangeville fall between $30,000 and $60,000 for a mid-range project with new cabinetry, countertops, and flooring. Budget refreshes start around $15,000, while full gut renovations with layout changes can exceed $65,000.

Is it cheaper to reface cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes, refacing is typically 30–50% less expensive than full cabinet replacement, since you're keeping the existing boxes and only replacing doors, drawer fronts, and hardware. It works well when your current layout functions well and the cabinet boxes are in good condition.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

A budget refresh can be completed in 1–2 weeks. A mid-range renovation typically takes 4–6 weeks, and a full gut renovation with layout changes can take 8–12 weeks depending on permit timelines and material lead times.
